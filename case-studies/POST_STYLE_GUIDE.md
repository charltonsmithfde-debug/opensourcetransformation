# LinkedIn Post Standards & Design Template

## 1. Post Text Principles
- **Length**: 8 lines at most (each line is a single punchy sentence separated by whitespace).
- **Tone**: Grounded, authentic business dilemma. No sales pitches, no "DM me", no aggressive CTA, no vendor jargon.
- **Narrative Structure**:
  1. Relatable business hook (e.g. founder outreach, real revenue scale).
  2. The financial reality / net profit context.
  3. The enterprise SaaS trap / exorbitant quote.
  4. The business threat (e.g., wiping out a third of profit).
  5. What the business actually needed (clarity, speed, reliability).
  6. The open-source / pragmatic solution & timeline (e.g. 5 days).
  7. Key operational proof (zero license fees, internal team enablement).
  8. Punchline / timeless business takeaway.

## 2. Branded Post Image Card (Tobi Oluwole Format)
- **Dimensions**: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5 portrait).
- **Background**: Solid black (`#000000`) or deep midnight.
- **Header**: Circular avatar (Charlton Smith), bold name, blue verified badge (`#1D9BF0`), subtle grey handle (`@charltonsmith`).
- **Typography**: Inter / clean modern sans-serif, bold (`font-weight: 600-700`), white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Rhythm**: 3 short, high-contrast statements with generous whitespace (50-60px gap).
  - Statement 1: The enterprise quote or industry absurdity.
  - Statement 2: The actual business constraint or profit reality.
  - Statement 3: The pragmatic takeaway or proof.
- **Tooling**: Rendered via headless Chromium at `1080x1350`.
