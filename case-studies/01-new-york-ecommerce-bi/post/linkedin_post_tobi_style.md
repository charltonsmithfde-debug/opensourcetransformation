### Copy for Post:

A New York e-commerce founder DM’d me last week.

His store does $7M in sales.

Sounds impressive.

Until he told me his net profit is in the low hundreds of thousands.

He was bleeding margin.

His biggest headache? 

Cart abandonment was spiking, and he had no idea why.

He didn’t have a BI team.

He was running the company on default Shopify exports and Amazon Seller Central CSVs.

Every vendor he spoke to quoted him the exact same playbook:

Snowflake. dbt Cloud. Fivetran. Power BI.

Price tag: $210,000/year in SaaS. 

Plus another $160,000 for a data engineer in New York to maintain it.

That alone would have wiped out his entire year’s profit.

He had no budget for software licenses. 

He didn’t need enterprise hype. 

He needed answers before his next board meeting.

I told him: “Give me 5 days.”

We didn’t buy a single software license.

We didn’t hire anyone new.

We set up an out-of-the-box architecture using Google Cloud Storage, DuckDB, Cube, and Metabase.

In 5 days, he had a live executive dashboard scanning his entire order history in 1.11 seconds.

Total software cost: $0.

Cloud hosting: under $100/month.

Next week, we are upskilling his 2 existing technical staff to maintain it.

I hope his board gives approval for this to be a full case study I can share with you.
