## Slide 2: The Siren Song
* **Eyebrow**: CHAPTER 1: THE PROMISE
* **Title**: "We Need to Modernize Our Data."
* **The Story**:
  It starts with the best intentions. 
  
  The board hears that the old systems are holding the company back. Mainstream consultancies arrive with glossy slides, promising that the "Modern Data Stack" will turn the business into an agile, data-driven machine.
  
  Everyone is flocking to Snowflake. It's the hot cloud platform. 
  
  So you sign the contract, commit to an estimated annual usage credit, and assume the hard part is over.

---

## Slide 3: The Hidden Escalation
* **Eyebrow**: CHAPTER 2: THE RUNAWAY TAX
* **Title**: First Came the Reskilling. Then the Consultants.
* **The Story**:
  Suddenly, your existing team can't manage this new environment on their own. 
  
  You need specialized cloud data engineers. You bring in high-rate consultants to set up pipelines. 
  
  Then comes the software bloat: paying thousands of dollars a year for commercial cloud licenses of tools (like dbt) that are essentially paid wrappers around everyday SQL scripts.
  
  What was pitched as a simple cloud migration is now an ongoing payroll and subscription sinkhole.

---

## Slide 4: The Safe Excuse
* **Eyebrow**: CHAPTER 3: THE SEAT TAX
* **Title**: "Nobody Ever Got Fired for Buying Microsoft."
* **The Story**:
  Next, you need to show the data to the team. 
  
  So you buy Power BI. Because it feels safe. "Nobody ever gets fired for buying Microsoft," right?
  
  Except now, there's a tollbooth:
  • Want your warehouse managers to see inventory turnover? That’s $14/month per person.
  • Want 80 frontline supervisors and sales reps to see daily metrics? Pay up.
  • Want to stop paying per-seat? Microsoft Fabric capacity starts at ~$60,000/year.
  
  So you start rationing access. Frontline staff are told they don't have a license, and they go right back to emailing stale Excel sheets.

## Slide 5: The Inevitable Day of Reckoning
* **Eyebrow**: CHAPTER 4: THE BOARD MEETING
* **Title**: Month 12: Finance Asks the Awkward Question.
* **The Story**:
  The invoices have been landing on the CFO’s desk month after month.
  
  The Snowflake compute bill fluctuates wildly because an unoptimized query or morning dashboard refresh kept the virtual meters spinning.
  
  The total spend is 2x to 3x what was budgeted.
  
  In private, executives quietly whisper: *"Honestly... things were simpler and cheaper on the old stack."*

---

## Slide 6: The Justification Trap
* **Eyebrow**: CHAPTER 5: THE INVENTED ROI
* **Title**: When You Can't See the Value, You Invent "Intangibles."
* **The Story**:
  Look around the business. Are shipments leaving faster? Are supplier penalty fines lower? Are margins clearer?
  
  Often... no.
  
  So the tech champions start inventing intangible benefits to justify the spend to the Board:
  • *"We are future-proofed for AI."*
  • *"Our semantic layer is decoupled."*
  • *"We have enterprise cloud governance."*
  
  It sounds impressive in a slide deck. But it doesn't add a single dollar to the bottom line.

## Slide 7: The Uncomfortable Truth
* **Eyebrow**: CHAPTER 6: THE MIRROR TEST
* **Title**: Did We Build This for the Business... Or to Make People Feel Important?
* **The Story**:
  Complex architectures make certain consultants and technical vendors look essential and feel sophisticated. 
  
  They lock you into proprietary ecosystems where leaving feels impossible.
  
  But true innovation isn't making simple things complicated. 
  
  True innovation is making powerful things simple, inexpensive, and accessible to everyone in your business.

## Slide 8: Being Brave & Different
* **Eyebrow**: CHAPTER 7: THE 7 PILLARS
* **Title**: What Happens When You Do What Competitors Won't?
* **The Story**:
  All you need is the courage to step off the vendor treadmill. 
  
  A modern data platform should follow 7 common-sense business rules:
  1. **Faster**: Answers in milliseconds, not minutes.
  2. **Cheaper**: 80–90% lower operational costs.
  3. **Measurable**: Clear, tangible dollar ROI on day one.
  4. **For Everyone**: Zero per-seat viewer taxes. 100 people or 1,000 people cost the exact same.
  5. **Zero Lock-In**: Open file formats. You can change or throw it out with zero friction.
  6. **Open Source**: You own your data and your code.
  7. **Simple to Support**: Any competent mid-level technician can manage it without six-figure consultants.
